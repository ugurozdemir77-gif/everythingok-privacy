Everything OK - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Overview

Everything OK is a personal safety and check-in application. No user account is required. The application may store the user’s name and telephone number (if provided), emergency contacts’ names and telephone numbers, schedules, settings, optional notes, and check-in and alert status locally in encrypted storage on the device.

Data and purpose

The app may access precise location, including in the background or while the app is closed, only to send an emergency location notification configured by the user. It may use SMS and telephone calling functions to contact selected emergency contacts. If location sharing is enabled, an SMS may include a location link. This information is not sent to the developer and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Sharing

When an alert is triggered, information required for the selected action may be processed by the Android system and mobile operator and delivered to the emergency contacts selected by the user. A location SMS may include a Google Maps link containing coordinates. If the recipient opens the link, Google may process that request under its own privacy terms. The developer does not sell personal data.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on the device until the user edits it, uses the in-app Delete My Data function, clears the application’s data, or uninstalls the app. The developer does not keep a server-side copy of this local information.

Security and choices

Sensitive settings are stored locally in encrypted storage backed by Android Keystore. The application package identifier is com.ugurozdemir.everythingok. The developer does not operate a server that receives emergency-contact, SMS, call, or location data. Android permissions can be changed in system settings; disabling required permissions may prevent related features from working.

Emergency limitation

Everything OK does not replace the police, ambulance, fire brigade, medical providers, or other professional emergency services. Test every configured notification channel before relying on the application.

Emergency-contact consent and delivery

If a check-in is not confirmed in time or the Emergency feature is used, the application may send SMS messages or place telephone calls to your saved emergency contacts through the channels you select. If location notifications are enabled, the device’s location may be sent to these contacts. Inform these people before adding them to the application and confirm that they agree to receive SMS messages, calls, and location sharing. Delivery depends on the device, permissions, mobile network, and carrier; successful delivery or intervention is not guaranteed. The application is not a substitute for official emergency services.

Children and policy changes

The application is not specifically designed for children and does not use personal data for profiling or targeted advertising. This policy may be updated if the application’s features or legal requirements change. The latest update date appears above.
