Everything OK - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Overview

Everything OK is a personal safety and check-in application. No user account is required. The application may store the user’s name and telephone number (if provided), emergency contacts’ names and telephone numbers, schedules, settings, optional notes, and check-in and alert status locally in encrypted storage on the device.

Data and purpose

The app may access precise location, including in the background or while the app is closed, only to send an emergency location notification configured by the user. It may use SMS and telephone calling functions to contact selected emergency contacts. If location sharing is enabled, an SMS may include a location link. This information is not sent to the developer and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Sharing

When an alert is triggered, information required for the selected action may be processed by the Android system and mobile operator and delivered to the emergency contacts selected by the user. A location SMS may include a Google Maps link containing coordinates. If the recipient opens the link, Google may process that request under its own privacy terms. The developer does not sell personal data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app may display banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information such as the device IP address (which may indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics and performance data, and device/app identifiers (including the advertising ID where available).

This advertising data may be processed to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where permitted and subject to applicable choices or consent, personalize advertising. Processing, retention and international transfers by Google and its partners are governed by their respective policies. The app does not intentionally send the locally saved emergency-contact names or numbers, SMS content, check-in notes, or precise emergency GPS coordinates to Google AdMob for ad targeting.

Advertising choices, consent and sponsors

Where required, the app uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request advertising choices. When the app displays a privacy-options control, you can reopen it to review or change those choices. You can also manage advertising privacy settings on your Android device and in your Google account. Declining personalized ads does not necessarily stop all ads or all technical data processing. The appearance and availability of a consent form depend on your region and the configured AdMob privacy message.

The app may display sponsored logos or banners managed through a remote configuration file. Opening a sponsor link may take you to a third-party website or app, which processes your visit under its own privacy policy. Fetching the remote configuration and images may disclose normal network information such as your IP address to the hosting service. These sponsorship and configuration requests do not intentionally include saved emergency-contact details or precise emergency GPS coordinates.

For questions about personal data or applicable rights of access, correction, deletion or objection, contact the developer using the address below. The in-app “Delete My Data” function deletes locally saved app data; it does not automatically erase data independently processed by Google or other third parties. For information about Google data and advertising, see:

Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data in partner apps · Google My Ad Center

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on the device until the user edits it, uses the in-app Delete My Data function, clears the application’s data, or uninstalls the app. The developer does not keep a server-side copy of this local information.

Security and choices

Sensitive settings are stored locally in encrypted storage backed by Android Keystore. The application package identifier is com.ugurozdemir.everythingok. The developer does not operate a server that receives emergency-contact, SMS, call, or location data. Android permissions can be changed in system settings; disabling required permissions may prevent related features from working.

Emergency limitation

Everything OK does not replace the police, ambulance, fire brigade, medical providers, or other professional emergency services. Test every configured notification channel before relying on the application.

Emergency-contact consent and delivery

If a check-in is not confirmed in time or the Emergency feature is used, the application may send SMS messages or place telephone calls to your saved emergency contacts through the channels you select. If location notifications are enabled, the device’s location may be sent to these contacts. Inform these people before adding them to the application and confirm that they agree to receive SMS messages, calls, and location sharing. Delivery depends on the device, permissions, mobile network, and carrier; successful delivery or intervention is not guaranteed. The application is not a substitute for official emergency services.

Children and policy changes

The app is not specifically designed for children. Locally stored emergency-contact and check-in information is not used by the developer for advertising targeting; the separate Google AdMob SDK may process advertising-related information as described above. This policy may be updated as features or legal requirements change; the latest update date appears above.

Everything OK – Privacybeleid

Laatst bijgewerkt: 3 november 2026

Overzicht

Everything OK is een app voor persoonlijke veiligheid en periodieke controles. Een account is niet nodig. Naam en telefoonnummer van de gebruiker (indien ingevoerd), namen en nummers van noodcontacten, schema’s, instellingen, optionele notities en bevestigings- en alarmstatus kunnen lokaal versleuteld worden opgeslagen.

Gegevens en doel

De app kan de nauwkeurige locatie gebruiken, ook op de achtergrond of wanneer de app gesloten is, uitsluitend voor een ingestelde noodlocatiemelding. De app kan sms en telefoongesprekken gebruiken om geselecteerde noodcontacten te bereiken. Als locatiedeling is ingeschakeld, kan een sms een locatielink bevatten. Deze gegevens worden niet aan de ontwikkelaar verzonden en niet gebruikt voor advertenties of analyses.

Delen

Bij een alarm kunnen Android en de mobiele provider de benodigde informatie verwerken en naar gekozen noodcontacten sturen. Een locatie-sms kan een Google Maps-link met coördinaten bevatten. Als de ontvanger deze opent, kan Google het verzoek volgens zijn eigen privacyvoorwaarden verwerken. De ontwikkelaar verkoopt geen persoonsgegevens.

Advertenties (Google AdMob)

De app kan banneradvertenties weergeven via Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Als advertenties zijn ingeschakeld, kunnen Google en advertentiepartners automatisch gegevens verzamelen of ontvangen, zoals het IP-adres van het apparaat (waaruit een globale locatie kan worden afgeleid), interacties met de app en advertenties, diagnostische en prestatiegegevens en apparaat- of app-ID’s (waaronder de advertentie-ID indien beschikbaar).

Deze gegevens kunnen worden verwerkt om advertenties te tonen en te meten, fraude en misbruik te voorkomen en, waar de wet en de toepasselijke keuzes of vereiste toestemming dit toelaten, advertenties te personaliseren. Bewaartermijnen en internationale doorgiften door Google en partners vallen onder hun eigen beleid. De app stuurt lokaal opgeslagen namen en nummers van noodcontacten, sms-inhoud, notities of nauwkeurige GPS-coördinaten van noodmeldingen niet opzettelijk naar AdMob voor advertentietargeting.

Waar nodig gebruikt de app Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) om advertentiekeuzes te vragen. Als in de app een optie voor advertentieprivacy verschijnt, kunt u daar uw keuzes bekijken of wijzigen. U kunt advertenties ook beheren in de instellingen van Android en uw Google-account. Het weigeren van gepersonaliseerde advertenties stopt niet noodzakelijk alle advertenties of alle technische gegevensverwerking. Of een toestemmingsformulier verschijnt, hangt af van uw regio en de AdMob-configuratie.

Het ophalen van configuratie en afbeeldingen kan gebruikelijke netwerkgegevens, zoals uw IP-adres, aan de hostingdienst doorgeven, maar stuurt niet opzettelijk opgeslagen noodcontactgegevens of nauwkeurige nood-GPS-coördinaten mee.
